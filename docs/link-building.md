# Earning links (to raise Domain Authority)

Domain Authority (Moz) is calculated from **other websites linking to ours**: how many different sites, and how trusted they are. Changes to our own pages can't raise it directly. These pages make linking to us easy; the team's outreach is what earns the links.

## What the site now offers
- **/press**: press kit. Ready-made descriptions, logo, “Featured on Kanpur Ki Kahaani” badges (light/dark, SVG/PNG) with copy-paste HTML, and our best pages to link to.
- **/guides/distances-from-kanpur**: a citable reference table (road and straight-line distance, drive and train times, airports, best season) with a **CSV download** (CC BY 4.0, credit with a link) and Dataset markup for Google.
- **“Cite or link to this page”** boxes on the distance guide, Nearby, Celebrations, Culture & History and every blog post: one click copies a link or a citation.
- Syndication (admin → Syndication): every cross-posted copy links back to the original.

## Outreach checklist (do a few each week)
1. **Places we feature.** When a story mentions a café, hotel, shop, event or creator, message them the link and the badge code from /press. Featured places link back more often than anyone else.
2. **Colleges.** Ask our department/college and student clubs to list the project (links from `.ac.in` sites carry a lot of trust).
3. **Local and travel directories.** Kanpur city portals, UP tourism and heritage listings, travel-blog directories (e.g. IndiBlogger). Submit once, with the short description from /press.
4. **Press.** Pitch Kanpur editions of Dainik Jagran, Amar Ujala, Hindustan and Hindustan Times before festivals (Ganga Mela, Durga Puja, Dev Deepawali), offering the festival guide and quotes.
5. **Guest posts.** One article a month for an established India travel blog, with a link to the matching guide (e.g. the distance guide for “weekend trips from Kanpur”).
6. **Communities.** Answer Kanpur travel questions on Reddit and Quora when a guide genuinely answers them. Link only where it helps.
7. **Cross-post** every new story (DEV, Hashnode, Medium import, LinkedIn) from admin → Syndication, and record hand-made copies there.
8. **Track it.** Check Moz Link Explorer (free account) or Google Search Console → Links each month; note new linking sites in the team sheet.

## Never
Buying links, link exchanges, private blog networks, comment spam, or “DA boost” services. They often don't raise DA for long, and they can get the site penalised by Google, losing real traffic.

## Realistic expectations
A new site starts near DA 0–5. With steady outreach, low double digits within 6–12 months is a good result. DA 50 is the level of well-known publications and takes years and links from many hundreds of reputable sites. Moz updates DA roughly monthly.
